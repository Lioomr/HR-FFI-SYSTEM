# Research — FFI / The Line of Certainty

Research informed original design principles only. No imagery, video, layouts, client logos, or other assets from these references are included in this project.

- [CANALS](https://canals-amsterdam.com/) — Use one deliberate spatial story with chapters rather than a collection of unrelated panels.
- [MVRDV](https://www.mvrdv.com/) — Let large editorial labels and a strict hierarchy make broad project categories feel like one architectural system.
- [M+](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/) — Delay dense evidence until after the immersive opening so factual content arrives as a curated resolution.
- [Vitra](https://www.vitra.com/en-us/home) — Pair rich imagery with a calm, legible taxonomy; restraint can communicate breadth more powerfully than feature lists.
- [Aman](https://www.aman.com/hotels-and-resorts) — Use silence, generous image area, sparse copy, and measured transitions to make premium material feel confident.
- [Porsche Experience](https://experience.porsche.com/en) — Treat the route through the experience as a sequence of waypoints with an obvious direction of travel.
- [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com/) — Combine precise editorial language with tactile material studies to make hard systems feel human and considered.
- [GSAP ScrollTrigger](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/) — Drive individual pinned scenes from native-scroll progress and keep all state reversible.
- [North by Northwest title sequence](https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/north-by-northwest/) — Let lines, rules, and type share one coordinate system so the visual device is structural rather than decorative.

## Original application

The resulting direction is **Measured Horizon**: a Saudi construction world rendered as a controlled survey film. A single real-brand orange datum travels through ground, construction, MEP coordination, renewal, public realm, and evidence. This produces an original authored experience: broad FFI capability is represented by one accountable line of responsibility, while the profile supplies the verified proof layer.
