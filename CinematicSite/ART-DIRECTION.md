# FFI — The Line of Certainty

## Creative direction

**Measured Horizon** is an editorial-industrial architectural film for Fathi Fouad Itani Contracting Company (FFI). It is deliberately quiet, spatial, and exact: a grounded Saudi construction world of graphite, concrete, steel, glass, dry earth, and restrained vegetation. It is not a technology demo, property montage, or speculative future-city story.

The visual differentiator is one continuous FFI-orange datum. It begins as a survey mark on untouched ground and becomes each act’s organizing coordinate: level, grid, route, service pulse, renewal cut, landscape path, and horizon. The real FFI logo is only used as an implementation-layer brand asset; it is never supplied to a generative model.

### System

- **Archetype:** engineered quiet / editorial architecture.
- **Typography:** restrained engineered grotesk, condensed uppercase act names, tabular operational labels, clear compact body copy.
- **Palette:** graphite `#141516`, mineral `#8c8b86`, pale concrete `#d8d2c6`, dry earth `#866f58`, muted landscape `#65745a`, authentic FFI orange `#F6821F`.
- **Camera:** stable 24–28 mm architectural frames, low-to-medium camera height, warm raking Saudi side light, low-saturation film grain.
- **Motion:** a maximum of one meaningful physical event per shot. Slow track/dolly, rigid geometry, real material behavior. No cuts, morphing, neon, fake logos, people, client logos, or embedded typography.
- **Interface:** calibration rails, hairlines, sector labels, and coordinate marks inherit the same datum-line logic rather than becoming a dashboard.

## Quality allocation

The recorded balance before submission was **100.5 credits (Starter)**. The plan prioritizes a high-resolution hero and MEP coordination sequence—the two scenes where temporal stability and detail are most visible—and a third landscape motion plate. Actual preflights and submissions used Cinema Studio 3.0 with silent 4-second outputs: two 1080p scenes (40 credits each) and one 720p scene (20 credits), consuming 100 credits. The remaining acts are authored as full-resolution native still plates with deterministic browser choreography, not inferior substitute footage.

No costs were spent on image generation through Higgsfield. Exact preflights were run before submission, and each completed clip was visually inspected before runtime use. The detailed record, including the final 0.5-credit balance, is in `GENERATION-LOG.md`.
