# Penalties API

**Effective from:** `PENALTIES_EFFECTIVE_FROM`, default `2026-09-29` (release date). Generation is prospective and has no backfill. Historical attendance violations, notices, deductions, and finalized payroll remain readable and unchanged. New legacy attendance penalty rows cease at the cutover; existing attendance routes remain available.

## Policy and access

The bilingual catalog is shared across companies and seeded from the supplied executive-regulations schedule, printed pages 39–42. It contains 50 entries: work time `W01`–`W16`, work organization `O01`–`O18`, and worker conduct `B01`–`B16`. Every catalog row includes `source_page`, `source_row`, `levels`, `count_period`, and `automatic`. `automatic: true` rows are generated from attendance evidence and cannot be manually created. Occurrences count separately for each printed catalog code. Work-time rows 1–10 reset monthly, work-time rows 11–16 reset by the employee's penalty anniversary year (anchored to contract start, then hire date, then `PENALTIES_EFFECTIVE_FROM`; contract expiry never lengthens the year), and other rows are cumulative. Source schedule actions and monetary bases are stored in each level; unknown levels are rejected instead of extrapolated.

All endpoints require authentication and a valid `x-active-company-id` selector. Records are scoped to that company. HRManager and SystemAdmin may list and manage company records; other authenticated users can list and retrieve only records attached to their own employee profile. The `mine=true` list filter also restricts HR/SystemAdmin to their own records. Cross-company or non-owned record IDs are hidden with 404. HR writes additionally follow the existing company write guard.

## Routes and envelopes

Success responses use `{ "status": "success", "data": ... }`; validation errors use the shared error envelope. List pagination returns `data: { items, page, page_size, count, total_pages }`. Routes have the `/api/penalties/` base and trailing slash.

Catalog items return `code`, `category` (`work_time`, `work_organization`, `worker_conduct`), `title_en/ar`, `description_en/ar`, `count_period`, `levels`, `source_page`, `source_row`, `automatic`, `extra_wage_deduction`, and `auto_warning_extra_levels` (2 for W01/W02/W07 while automatic warnings are enabled, else 0). A level carries `occurrence`, `action`, `amount_basis`, and `amount_value`; `amount_basis` is `daily_wage_percent`, `daily_wage_days`, or null. `action` includes `written_warning`, `deduction`, and the source's named nonmonetary outcomes.

Record DTOs return `id`, `company_id`, `employee_profile_id`, `employee_name_en/ar`, `catalog_code`, `category`, `occurred_on`, `occurrence_number`, `count_period`, `action`, `amount`, `extra_wage_amount`, `total_deduction_amount`, `status`, `source`, `automation` (`""`, `warning_pending`, `warning_issued`), `warning_notice` (null or `{ id, reference_number, delivery_status, issued_at, download_path }`), `attendance_result_id`, `attendance_record_id`, `description`, `description_en/ar`, `note`, `evidence`, `employee_response`, `dispute_reason`, `resolution`, `payroll_status`, `source_page`, `source_row`, `created_at`, and `updated_at`. `description` is the English catalog description. `source` is `automatic` or `hr`; `note` is the incident or candidate note, and `evidence` is a JSON object. `employee_response` is null or `{ "decision": "acknowledged" \| "disputed", "reason": null \| string, "submitted_at": ISO timestamp }`. `dispute_reason` mirrors its reason only while the current response is disputed. `resolution` is null or a decision/note or reason/timestamp object reflecting HR marking, dispute resolution, excuse, or correction review. `payroll_status` is null without a monetary deduction.

## Status and payroll behavior

Record statuses are `pending_hr_mark`, `issued`, `disputed`, `waived`, and `applied`. Automatic attendance evidence creates a candidate at `pending_hr_mark`; HR confirms the schedule facts and permission/excuse before issue. The candidate is never payroll eligible. HR `excused` sets `waived` with an explicit `excused` resolution. Source nonmonetary actions remain records and do not execute termination or employment changes.

Deduction statuses are `pending_review`, `approved`, `held`, `claimed`, `applied`, and `void`. `amount` is the schedule sanction. `extra_wage_amount` is any printed additional wage withholding for measured time or absence. `total_deduction_amount` is their sum and the sole payroll claim amount. HR approval makes an issued, undisputed monetary record eligible; hold or an unresolved dispute blocks new claims. A draft payroll run claims approved amounts once and synchronizes its run item, payslip, and run total idempotently. Corrections or disputes release mutable draft claims. Finalization marks the claim and record applied and freezes the completed payroll. A later eligible record waits for a future draft; finalized runs are never recalculated.

[HRSD Labor Relations Article 70](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84) limits one disciplinary fine to five days' wages and the total fines deducted from one month's salary to five days' wages. The payroll guard uses the employee's current `total_salary / 30 × 5`, rounded to currency precision, consistently with the schedule's daily-wage calculation. It counts `PenaltyRecord.amount` and legacy `AttendancePayrollDeduction.claimed_amount` as fines across both claim services. `extra_wage_amount` for unworked time is separate and does not consume this fine budget. Each fine is claimed in full or deferred; the service never silently reduces the source sanction. An over-limit legacy claim stays pending, and an approved new claim that does not fit stays approved for a later draft. Within each claim service, already claimed rows are evaluated first, then remaining rows in stable ID order; the service called first reserves budget first. Normal payroll generation and finalization call legacy attendance sync before new penalty sync. Draft re-sync releases an existing claim if later evidence or the five-day budget makes it ineligible. Completed or paid runs remain immutable.

A dispute after application is record-only: `status` becomes `disputed`, while `payroll_status` stays `applied`, and no reversal or future claim is created. HR `uphold` restores record status `applied`; `waive` closes the record while leaving the completed deduction and payroll unchanged. A post-final attendance correction records `manual_review`, notifies HR, and leaves the applied state and payroll unchanged. Audit events record creation, issue, employee response, HR decisions, payroll reviews and claims, and corrections. Employee and HR notifications use the configured channel dispatcher with generic private summaries and secure company-linked app routes; violation details and notes are not sent to external channels.

## Automatic late warnings

**Deviation from the printed schedule (company policy, decided 2026-10-01):** for W01, W02 and W07 only, the first three occurrences in a month are written warnings; occurrence 4 onward uses the printed levels shifted by two (occurrence 4 = printed level 2, 5 = level 3, 6+ = level 4). The frozen catalog is unchanged; the rule lives in `penalties.services.AUTO_WARNING_ROWS` / `EXTRA_AUTO_WARNINGS` and is applied by `_rated_level`, so issue, `rerate` and backdated recurrence review rate identically.

It is off until `PENALTY_AUTO_WARNINGS_EFFECTIVE_FROM` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, default empty) is set, and applies only to incidents on or after that date (the shifted levels too). Invalid values fail system checks `penalties.E002` and `penalties.E003` (`PENALTY_AUTO_WARNING_SETTLE_HOURS`, default 12).

- A new W01/W02/W07 attendance candidate is created with `automation = warning_pending`. The hourly Celery task `penalties.tasks.issue_auto_warnings` (beat minute `PENALTY_AUTO_WARNING_MINUTE`, default 15) issues candidates whose shift ended at least the settle hours ago, per employee in date order under the profile lock. Occurrences 1–3 are issued without an HR mark (`resolution.decision = auto_warning`, `automation = warning_issued`); occurrence 4+ becomes a normal HR candidate (`automation = ""`). HR may waive afterwards.
- W07 keeps its unworked-time wage: an automatic W07 warning has `amount = 0` and a positive `extra_wage_amount`/`total_deduction_amount` that follows the normal payroll review. W01/W02 warnings have no deduction.
- Each warning gets a private PDF letter (`PWN-{company code}-{record id:06d}`) rendered on the approved level 1 late-notice template; occurrence and percentage fields are blank and the reason is a fixed Arabic system statement. The employee receives an in-app notification and the `late_attendance_notice_v1` WhatsApp template with the PDF attached (its occurrence variable is `-`). Letter, notification and issue share one transaction: a render failure rolls back the issue; after three failures the candidate goes to HR (`resolution.auto_warning_failures`).
- HR list hiding: for HR/SystemAdmin without `mine=true`, the list (and therefore the status counts) excludes `warning_pending` records and `warning_issued` records unless HR has something to decide (pending again, disputed, `manual_review`, or a deduction in `pending_review`/`held`). `include_automated=true` or an `employee_profile_id` filter shows them; detail, actions and the download are never hidden. Employees always see their own warnings. `check_aged_penalties` ignores `warning_pending` candidates.
- Employees never see a count: for non-HR requesters, automatic records return `occurrence_number: null` and `resolution` limited to `decision`, `reason`, `note`, `resolved_at` and `reopened_at`.
- Waiving an issued automatic warning (HR resolve/waive, or automatic waiver when attendance evidence disappears) sends one "warning withdrawn" notification (in-app and the generic `request_status_update` WhatsApp template) instead of the generic resolved message. A withdrawn letter is never re-sent: if evidence returns, the reopened candidate goes to HR. HR marking a hidden candidate takes it out of automation and rates it as the marked row.
- A backdated warning renumbers later automatic warnings that stay warnings (audit `penalty_auto_warning_renumbered`); a later warning that would become a fine gets the normal recurrence correction review.

## Correction reviews

An issued, disputed, or applied record whose basis changes after issue is not re-rated automatically. It receives `resolution = { "decision": "manual_review", "reason", "proposed_evidence", "resolved_at", "previous_resolution", "held_from" }`, HR is notified, and a mutable deduction becomes `held` (`held_from` keeps its earlier status). Payroll approval returns 409 while the flag is set, and draft payroll does not claim it. Triggers are: changed same-kind attendance evidence; changed absence dates; an earlier unapplied absence-wage band being waived (only downstream rows that carry absence wages, so W15/W16 are skipped); and backdated or waived earlier incidents that change the chronological occurrence (`proposed_evidence.expected_occurrence`).

The flag clears in these ways:

- Automatic: when attendance evidence returns to exactly the issued snapshot (same band, date, and evidence), an unapplied record's flag clears, its previous resolution is restored, and a deduction held only by the review returns to `pending_review` (an HR payroll hold stays held). Audit: `penalty_evidence_review_cleared`.
- `waive`: closes the record; an applied deduction and finalized payroll are unchanged.
- `reopen`: only for an unapplied automatic record. The server recomputes the replacement from the current attendance result (late, early departure, after hours) or the current absence spell ending on the record date. It never uses a stored proposal. It returns 409 when current evidence establishes no candidate. It resets the record to `pending_hr_mark` with the current catalog row, evidence, and candidate note, voids the former mutable deduction, records `resolution = { "decision": "reopened", "note", "reopened_at" }`, audits the previous state (`penalty_correction_reopened`), and re-checks downstream recurrence.
- `rerate`: only for an unapplied, issued or disputed record whose review is recurrence-only (`expected_occurrence` present, no `catalog_code`). It re-rates `occurrence_number`, `action`, `amount`, and `total_deduction_amount` with the same recurrence, schedule-level, salary, and five-day rules as issue; extra wages and their date allocation are unchanged. It sets `resolution.decision = "recurrence_rerated"`, returns a deduction held only by the review to `pending_review` (disputed records stay held), audits previous amounts and resolution (`penalty_recurrence_rerated`), and re-checks downstream recurrence. It returns 409 for applied records or other review kinds, and 422 when a schedule rule rejects the new level. Employee responses are kept.

## Attendance generation

A day whose only punch is untyped, or is a single punch that is not a typed check-in, is flagged `calculation_inputs.lone_opaque_punch`. The attendance record keeps its projected check-in, but such a day creates no late-arrival, early-departure, or after-hours candidate, and existing unissued candidates for it are invalidated. Late arrival uses measured first check-in after shift start on a working day, independently of the legacy grace classifier; approved permission/excuse excludes it. Early departure, after-hours presence, and system absence create one idempotent candidate per source event or absence spell. W01–W06 retain their disrupted/not-disrupted branches. W07 is independent of disruption. For W08–W16, HR confirms unauthorized/unexcused facts and any required written warning. Continuous absence advances through W11 (one day), W12 (two to six), W13 (seven to ten), W14 (eleven to fourteen), and W15 (more than fifteen). The printed schedule says continuous **days**, without a working-day qualifier, and [HRSD Article 80](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84) likewise says more than fifteen consecutive days. The detector therefore measures elapsed calendar days from the first through the latest verified missed scheduled shift. Nonworking days may bridge a spell but cannot create absence evidence themselves. Candidate `evidence.continuous_calendar_days` drives the W12–W15 band; `evidence.absence_days` and `contract_year_absence_days` count missed scheduled shifts and drive any additional absence-period wage withholding and W16 intermittent count. `evidence.spell_start_on` records the first supporting missed shift. `evidence.absence_dates` lists the measured missed-shift dates in the spell; on issue, `evidence.wage_absence_dates` records only the dates not already covered by an earlier charge, and only those dates are withheld as absence wages. The printed continuous bands have no new action at exactly calendar day fifteen, but W16 still applies if intermittent contract-year missed shifts exceed thirty. Attendance recalculation and record/leave correction reconcile candidates against the current full spell; an issued row remains issued only while its catalog band is still supported. This detector does not infer an authorization or written warning from an attendance timestamp; HR must confirm whether off-days legally continue a particular absence spell before issuing it.
