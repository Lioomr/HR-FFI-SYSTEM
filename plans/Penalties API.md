# Penalties API

**Effective from:** `PENALTIES_EFFECTIVE_FROM`, default `2026-09-29` (release date). Generation is prospective and has no backfill. Historical attendance violations, notices, deductions, and finalized payroll remain readable and unchanged. New legacy attendance penalty rows cease at the cutover; existing attendance routes remain available.

## Policy and access

The bilingual catalog is shared across companies and seeded from the supplied executive-regulations schedule, printed pages 39–42. It contains 50 entries: work time `W01`–`W16`, work organization `O01`–`O18`, and worker conduct `B01`–`B16`. Every catalog row includes `source_page`, `source_row`, `levels`, `count_period`, and `automatic`. `automatic: true` rows are generated from attendance evidence and cannot be manually created. Occurrences count separately for each printed catalog code. Work-time rows 1–10 reset monthly, work-time rows 11–16 reset by the employee's contract-year cycle, and other rows are cumulative. Source schedule actions and monetary bases are stored in each level; unknown levels are rejected instead of extrapolated.

All endpoints require authentication and a valid `x-active-company-id` selector. Records are scoped to that company. HRManager and SystemAdmin may list and manage company records; other authenticated users can list and retrieve only records attached to their own employee profile. The `mine=true` list filter also restricts HR/SystemAdmin to their own records. Cross-company or non-owned record IDs are hidden with 404. HR writes additionally follow the existing company write guard.

## Routes and envelopes

Success responses use `{ "status": "success", "data": ... }`; validation errors use the shared error envelope. List pagination returns `data: { items, page, page_size, count, total_pages }`. Routes have the `/api/penalties/` base and trailing slash.

| Method and route | Actor | Request |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/penalties/catalog/` | Authenticated | No query parameters. `data` is the catalog array. |
| `GET /api/penalties/` | Authenticated | Optional `mine=true`, `status`, `category`, `employee_profile_id`, `date_from`, `date_to`, `search`, `page`, `page_size`. `status` and `category` accept comma-separated values. |
| `GET /api/penalties/{id}/` | Owner or HR/SystemAdmin | No body. |
| `POST /api/penalties/` | HR/SystemAdmin | `{ "employee_profile_id": integer, "catalog_code": "O01", "occurred_on": "YYYY-MM-DD", "note": "..." }`. Only non-automatic catalog rows, dated from cutover through today. Returns 201. |
| `POST /api/penalties/{id}/mark-disruption/` | HR/SystemAdmin | `{ "disruption": "disrupted" \| "not_disrupted" \| "confirmed" \| "excused", "note": "..." }`. For W01–W06, use disrupted, not_disrupted, or excused. For W07–W16, use confirmed or excused. `confirmed` issues that row unchanged. `excused` waives it without a deduction. |
| `POST /api/penalties/{id}/acknowledge/` | Employee owner | No body. Non-attendance issued or applied record; records receipt. |
| `POST /api/penalties/{id}/dispute/` | Employee owner | `{ "reason": "..." }`. Non-attendance issued or applied record. May follow acknowledgement; the new response replaces the current acknowledgement, which remains in audit history. |
| `POST /api/penalties/{id}/resolve/` | HR/SystemAdmin | `{ "decision": "uphold" \| "waive", "note": "..." }` for a disputed record. |
| `POST /api/penalties/{id}/payroll-review/` | HR/SystemAdmin | `{ "decision": "approve" \| "hold", "note": "..." }` for an issued monetary record. |

Catalog items return `code`, `category` (`work_time`, `work_organization`, `worker_conduct`), `title_en/ar`, `description_en/ar`, `count_period`, `levels`, `source_page`, `source_row`, `automatic`, and `extra_wage_deduction`. A level carries `occurrence`, `action`, `amount_basis`, and `amount_value`; `amount_basis` is `daily_wage_percent`, `daily_wage_days`, or null. `action` includes `written_warning`, `deduction`, and the source's named nonmonetary outcomes.

Record DTOs return `id`, `company_id`, `employee_profile_id`, `employee_name_en/ar`, `catalog_code`, `category`, `occurred_on`, `occurrence_number`, `count_period`, `action`, `amount`, `extra_wage_amount`, `total_deduction_amount`, `status`, `source`, `attendance_result_id`, `attendance_record_id`, `description`, `description_en/ar`, `note`, `evidence`, `employee_response`, `dispute_reason`, `resolution`, `payroll_status`, `source_page`, `source_row`, `created_at`, and `updated_at`. `description` is the English catalog description. `source` is `automatic` or `hr`; `note` is the incident or candidate note, and `evidence` is a JSON object. `employee_response` is null or `{ "decision": "acknowledged" \| "disputed", "reason": null \| string, "submitted_at": ISO timestamp }`. `dispute_reason` mirrors its reason only while the current response is disputed. `resolution` is null or a decision/note or reason/timestamp object reflecting HR marking, dispute resolution, excuse, or correction review. `payroll_status` is null without a monetary deduction.

## Status and payroll behavior

Record statuses are `pending_hr_mark`, `issued`, `disputed`, `waived`, and `applied`. Automatic attendance evidence creates a candidate at `pending_hr_mark`; HR confirms the schedule facts and permission/excuse before issue. The candidate is never payroll eligible. HR `excused` sets `waived` with an explicit `excused` resolution. Source nonmonetary actions remain records and do not execute termination or employment changes.

Deduction statuses are `pending_review`, `approved`, `held`, `claimed`, `applied`, and `void`. `amount` is the schedule sanction. `extra_wage_amount` is any printed additional wage withholding for measured time or absence. `total_deduction_amount` is their sum and the sole payroll claim amount. HR approval makes an issued, undisputed monetary record eligible; hold or an unresolved dispute blocks new claims. A draft payroll run claims approved amounts once and synchronizes its run item, payslip, and run total idempotently. Corrections or disputes release mutable draft claims. Finalization marks the claim and record applied and freezes the completed payroll. A later eligible record waits for a future draft; finalized runs are never recalculated.

[HRSD Labor Relations Article 70](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84) limits one disciplinary fine to five days' wages and the total fines deducted from one month's salary to five days' wages. The payroll guard uses the employee's current `total_salary / 30 × 5`, rounded to currency precision, consistently with the schedule's daily-wage calculation. It counts `PenaltyRecord.amount` and legacy `AttendancePayrollDeduction.claimed_amount` as fines across both claim services. `extra_wage_amount` for unworked time is separate and does not consume this fine budget. Each fine is claimed in full or deferred; the service never silently reduces the source sanction. An over-limit legacy claim stays pending, and an approved new claim that does not fit stays approved for a later draft. Within each claim service, already claimed rows are evaluated first, then remaining rows in stable ID order; the service called first reserves budget first. Normal payroll generation and finalization call legacy attendance sync before new penalty sync. Draft re-sync releases an existing claim if later evidence or the five-day budget makes it ineligible. Completed or paid runs remain immutable.

A dispute after application is record-only: `status` becomes `disputed`, while `payroll_status` stays `applied`, and no reversal or future claim is created. HR `uphold` restores record status `applied`; `waive` closes the record while leaving the completed deduction and payroll unchanged. A post-final attendance correction records `manual_review`, notifies HR, and leaves the applied state and payroll unchanged. Audit events record creation, issue, employee response, HR decisions, payroll reviews and claims, and corrections. Employee and HR notifications use the configured channel dispatcher with generic private summaries and secure company-linked app routes; violation details and notes are not sent to external channels.

## Attendance generation

Late arrival uses measured first check-in after shift start on a working day, independently of the legacy grace classifier; approved permission/excuse excludes it. Early departure, after-hours presence, and system absence create one idempotent candidate per source event or absence spell. W01–W06 retain their disrupted/not-disrupted branches. W07 is independent of disruption. For W08–W16, HR confirms unauthorized/unexcused facts and any required written warning. Continuous absence advances through W11 (one day), W12 (two to six), W13 (seven to ten), W14 (eleven to fourteen), and W15 (more than fifteen). The printed schedule has no new action at exactly day fifteen. Intermittent contract-year total above thirty uses W16. Attendance recalculation and record/leave correction reconcile candidates. This detector does not infer an authorization or written warning from an attendance timestamp.
